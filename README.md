This program was created with the firm purpose of helping the community of machinists, programmers, and CNC students. My intention is not profit, but to provide a free tool that simplifies the calculation and validation of arc geometries, preventing machine errors and helping beginners better understand G-code.
Development Note: This software is the result of a modern collaborative process: it was guided by my experience and technical vision, and developed with the support of Artificial Intelligence to ensure precision in mathematical calculations.
Main Features:
Exact calculation in modes: Radius (R), Offsets (I/J), and Absolute Center (Cx/Cy).
Major and Minor Arc Selection.
Integrity Assistant: The program automatically detects "impossible arcs" and suggests the necessary correction so the machine doesn't trigger alarms.
I hope this tool is very useful to you in the workshop or in your learning. If it helps you, share it with other colleagues!
"Technology is best when it brings people together and makes everyone's work easier."
